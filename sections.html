    Drum Major

    The drum major is the leader of the marching band. They are responsible for conducting the band during performances, leading rehearsals, and serving as a liaison between the band and the director. Drum majors must have excellent leadership skills, a strong sense of timing, and the ability to inspire and motivate their fellow band members.
